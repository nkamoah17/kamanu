KAMANU hallmark registry

House rules

What is done, as of the build on 2026-11-26:

  • The vendor sees a result first: a hallmark that names someone else's engine is sent to its maintainer before it is shown here.
  • The owner decides when it is shown: by default a vendor's result is held until its hold has passed after the vendor received it, and the owner may release it earlier, on the record, naming who, the date and the reason.
  • Rule E5: held unless released on the record. A released result's page says so in one line: the date the vendor received it, that it was published before the hold was up by the owner's decision, and whether the vendor has answered.
  • Every figure is computed by the assay and never typed in (rule E2); a hallmark that lapses is shown as lapsed and is never removed.

The hold for this build is 30 days after the vendor received the result.

The mint's words

Each word below means one exact thing, and the registry, the Catalogue and the printed hallmark use it for that alone (generated from kamanu.assay.mint.TABLE; the same list is docs/mint.md).

face value
the cap: the most the limit is set to let through, in the policy's own units
assay
the run that gives the ceiling: the most that can really be spent under the limit (lint's exposure on the scope ledger)
fineness
face value over ceiling, shown as a millesimal mark with three decimals (.500, 1.000), never rounded up
fine
fineness 1.000 at the hallmark's level, and only then
debased
fineness below 1.000, shown with the amount by which it is: the ceiling less the face value, in the limit's own units (unbounded when the ceiling is)
stranded budget
the most free headroom left when a request that would fit is refused (lint's worst Tight figure)
hallmark
the signed record of one assay: its figures, levels, hypotheses and hashes
mint mark
the hashes of the model (the policy and its lint form), the profile and the Lean sources
struck
the date of the run that issued the hallmark
obverse
the face of a hallmark: face value, fineness, stranded budget and level
reverse
the back of a hallmark: the hypotheses, each proved, measured or assumed, and the trace
touchstone
the coach: the product that checks a payment before it is made (does it fit, the most that fits, and when the full amount will)
countermark
the connector assessment: the product that checks that no caller reaches another tenant's money or records through a connector
pyx
the re-measurement of a hallmark, kept as signed entries beside it
trial of the pyx
the launch: the hallmarks shown in public with their re-measurement
lapsed
a hallmark whose re-measurement failed or ran out (past its valid-through date)
