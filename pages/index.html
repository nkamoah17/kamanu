What you get

Try it in a minute

The record above, computed on your own machine. Python 3.11 or later; nothing is uploaded, and nothing here needs an account.

pip install kamanu
kamanu scan --example per-charge

That reads a Stripe Issuing card capped at 5000 on each charge and nothing else, and says so: four charges of 5000 each pass it, 20000 in all, and nothing stops a fifth. It keeps no receipt and exits 2, because nothing was checked.

kamanu scan --example budget

That is the record above: a spend cap of 100 on the engine our proof covers. It prints the ceiling beside the limit, the stranded budget, the six-step trace, and on its last line how many of the engine's constants it had to assume. It exits 1, because one check fails. Run it on your own policy, or read a card issuer's spending_limits the way its documents write them:

kamanu lint examples/controls.yaml --frontend issuing --engine reference

A daily limit is checked as a sliding 24-hour window, and the answer says what the calendar reading, a reset at midnight UTC, could change: up to twice the amount across one boundary.

The same check runs on every pull request with the GitHub Action: uses: nkamoah17/kamanu@v0.1.

How sure

Every claim carries one of seven levels, and a result is only as strong as its weakest link.

LevelWhat it says
MODEL_PROVENEvery step from the kernel to the checked machine is proved in Lean.
EXHAUSTIVE_FINITE_PASSEvery reachable state was visited. One link is tested, not proved.
BOUNDED_PASSIt passed within stated bounds, and says nothing beyond them.
TESTEDA live run or a replay passed.
ASSUMEDIt passed, and something it rests on is a default or a reading of documents.
REFUTEDA counterexample stands.
UNKNOWNNot decided.

How a limit is measured

We run a fixed battery on one authorization path, in your sandbox or test mode. Each test runs 99 times. That catches a race that happens 3 times in 100 or more, 95 times out of 100. A rarer race can still be there, and the report says so.

  • Single asks at, below and above the limit.
  • Parallel asks, at several spacings, and several cards under one agent or cardholder.
  • Authorization, partial capture and reversal, including captures above the hold.
  • Ambiguous failure. A time-out and a retry, where the provider fails after the money has moved, or before, or does not answer.
  • Reset, expiry and overlapping limits. What happens at the edge of each window.

The report keeps three parts apart. Model: what can happen under the stated assumptions. Measurement: what happened in the tested environment, with trial counts. Deployment: what connects the tested configuration to the live one.

Three payment rails were measured this way on test networks on 24 and 25 November 2026: a client-side ceiling on each payment, an on-chain spending allowance and a cloud payment-session budget. Each limit held in every test. Each rail is named here when its owner has seen the result and agreed.

Verify it yourself

You do not have to take our word for the proofs. Clone the repository and run one script.

git clone https://github.com/nkamoah17/kamanu.git kamanu-src
cd kamanu-src
bash verify.sh

It builds the Lean proofs from clean, rejects any gap and any axiom beyond Lean's three standard ones, replays them with Lean's own checker, and has three outside checkers read an export. The theorems are listed in plain words.

And the record, offline, with the package or with node alone:

kamanu assay --verify registry/h/b271f39d07877083.json \
    --key registry/registry.pub --recompute
node registry/kamanu-verify.cjs registry/h/b271f39d07877083.html \
    "$(cat registry/registry.pub)"

The registry's public key is 7fae4269ca9e431336a566f036d10f8cc2e6702c03af12fbccef3264dd926eab. A record signed with any other key is not one of ours. Anyone can sign a record of their own with the same software and their own key; only this key says KAMANU struck it.

Get in touch

What this is not

  • It is not a certificate. We do not use the words "certified" or "safe".
  • It is not runtime enforcement. KAMANU never holds or moves money.
  • It is not a statement about your system unless it was measured there. A proof is about a model, and it reaches a real system only through the hypotheses printed beside it.

House rules

  • The owner of a system sees a result about it first, and has 30 days before anything is public.
  • A fee never changes a verdict or its timing.
  • Every public number carries its level and its hypotheses.
  • We test only in our own accounts and sandboxes, or with written permission.
  • Passes are published as readily as failures.
