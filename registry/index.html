KAMANU hallmark registry

Each row is a signed hallmark: a limit's face value (its cap), the ceiling the assay gives for it, its fineness (face value over ceiling, a millesimal mark with three decimals, never rounded up), its stranded budget and the proof level of its weakest link, shown with the hypotheses (each measured, assumed or proved) and the trace behind them. Each page shows the whole record, its mint mark (the hashes), the day it was struck and every re-measurement in its Pyx. A hallmark whose re-measurement changed a figure, lowered a level or ran past its valid-through date is shown as LAPSED, with the date and the reason; it is never removed.

A limit is called fine only at fineness 1.000, at its level; below 1.000 it is called debased, by the ceiling less the face value, and always beside its trace and its level (rule E1).

Built on 2026-11-28 from the records alone. A record of someone else's engine is held unless released on the record (rule E5): it is left out until its hold has passed on the day of the build, or until the owner releases it on the record; a private record is left out.
